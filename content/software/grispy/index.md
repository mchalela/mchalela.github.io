**Role:** lead developer and project manager of a team of five. **Code:** [mchalela/GriSPy](https://github.com/mchalela/GriSPy)

GriSPy is a regular grid search algorithm for quick nearest-neighbour lookup, focused on query time for large astronomical catalogues. It supports periodic boundary conditions and several distance metrics, including spherical ones.

I designed the package, wrote most of the core, and set up the test suite, CI and documentation. The method and its benchmarks are described in a paper in *Astronomy & Computing*.
