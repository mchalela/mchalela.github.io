**Role:** developer. **Code:** [Gaiana/nirdust](https://github.com/Gaiana/nirdust)

Nirdust fits near-infrared K-band spectra to measure the temperature of the dust heated by an active galactic nucleus. I implemented the parametric model optimisation, the tests and the documentation. The package is described in a paper in *MNRAS*.
